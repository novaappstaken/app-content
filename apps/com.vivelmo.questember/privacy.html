Privacy Policy for Remote: Cast Games Videos

Last updated: October 2026

Remote: Cast Games Videos ("we", "our", or "the app") provides virtual gamepad emulation, retro gaming, local media casting, and remote navigation features. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The app is designed to function locally and offline. We do not require an account, username, password, or email to use any part of the application. - Local Device Storage: Your preferences, game high scores, and saved device connection history are stored locally on your device in a secure private Room database. - Media Files: When you choose to cast photos, videos, or music to your Smart TV, the app accesses files from your device's media storage only upon your explicit action. These files are streamed strictly over your local Wi-Fi network directly to your television and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • Google AdMob: For free users, the app displays advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads SDK. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and crash logs to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising and prevent fraud. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing: When you upgrade to Remote Pro, the transaction is processed directly through Google Play Billing. We do not store or have access to your payment card details or financial credentials.
  • Pro Users: Upgrading to Remote Pro completely removes all advertising SDK requests and ad tracking.

3. Permissions

The app requests permissions only when necessary to perform its functions: - INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Wi-Fi discovery of smart TVs and streaming local media. - CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Required to scan for local UPnP/DLNA devices on your home Wi-Fi network. - VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback when pressing gamepad buttons. - READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: Used solely to select media you choose to cast to your TV.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All local data can be cleared at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or by using the Reset Data option in the app Settings.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store developer listing.
