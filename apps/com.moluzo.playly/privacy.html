Game Mode privacy policy

Updated 27 October 2026

Game Mode stores session profiles, selected app package names, pinned apps, session times, permission preferences and prior settings locally. Android device statistics are read for the screen and are not sent to a developer server. There is no account, login or developer backend. The installed-app list is used locally for the game hub.

Optional system settings access allows brightness changes; Do Not Disturb access allows focus controls, which do not reject calls. Notification permission is used only for optional session reminders. The app does not read contacts, messages or game files. Return and end an active session to restore its previous settings before removing the app.

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob after the consent flow allows ad requests. The advertising SDK may collect/share device and advertising identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad/app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Network transmission uses TLS. Ad controls are available under Ad privacy choices and Android advertising settings. See https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google's handling of advertising data.

Google Play handles payments and purchase history. The app receives purchase records/tokens to check entitlements and acknowledge purchases, and never receives your payment card details. Only a confirmed purchased product enables Pro; rewarded ads unlock one selected feature temporarily. Pro stops ad requests.

JSON and CSV exports are written only to the location you choose. An export may include profile names, app package names and session times. Copies outside the app are controlled by you and the selected storage provider. Import does not upload your data to us.

Clear session history in Settings to remove local session records. Clear app storage or uninstall to delete remaining local app data. Delete exported copies yourself. Manage subscriptions in Google Play; uninstalling does not cancel them. Ad identifiers can be reset/deleted in Android settings. We do not operate a server holding your profiles and do not offer account creation.

The app is intended as a general device utility and is not directed to children. Future changes will update this policy at the same address.
