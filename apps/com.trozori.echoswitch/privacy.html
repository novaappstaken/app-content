Privacy Policy - Status Saver: Video

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Status Saver ("the app", package com.trozori.echoswitch) helps you save photo and video statuses you have already viewed in your messenger. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles.

What stays on your phone

  • Statuses and saved files. The app reads the status folder you choose in the system file picker and copies the files you save into your gallery (Pictures/Status Saver and Movies/Status Saver). These files never leave your device through us.
  • Saved list and settings. A small local database and settings file (theme, folder access, auto-save) are stored only on your device. Exports you create are saved where you choose.
  • Direct chat. The phone number and message you type are only used to open the chat link you request; we do not store or send them anywhere else.

We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not ask for an account.

Permissions

  • Folder access (Android 10 and later): granted by you in the system picker, limited to the status folder you select. You can remove it in Settings > Reset folder access.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older): used only to read the status folder and save copies.
  • Internet: used for ads, the consent form and Google Play purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, diagnostics and ad interaction data. Where consent is required we ask for it with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Pro users do not see ads and no ads are loaded for them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pro is sold through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google; the app only receives confirmation that a purchase exists so it can unlock Pro. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything the app stored. Saved media remains in your gallery until you delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always at https://novaappstaken.github.io/app-content/apps/com.trozori.echoswitch/privacy.html

Status Saver is an independent app and is not affiliated with any messaging service.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact Nova Apps through the developer contact on the app's Google Play page.
