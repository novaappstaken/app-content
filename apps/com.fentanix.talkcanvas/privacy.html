Privacy Policy - Call Screen Theme: Wallpapers

Last updated: October 2026

Call Screen Theme: Wallpapers ("we", "our", or "the app") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Advertising Data: The app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve ads according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • In-App Purchases: All purchases and subscriptions are handled directly through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or process payment card details.
  • Contacts Information: If you grant contact read permission, the app accesses your contacts strictly locally on your device to allow you to associate specific call wallpapers with particular callers. No contact information ever leaves your device or is transmitted across the network.
  • Camera Flash: The app utilizes camera hardware permissions exclusively to operate the device LED torch as an incoming call alert light. No camera images or video are ever recorded, accessed, or saved.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play Services: - Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Google Play Billing: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All user preferences (theme selections, custom buttons, flash speed) are stored locally in private application storage. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@fentanix.com.
